# Go-to-market launch plan

This picks up where the `roveboard` skill's GTM playbook (validation, design-partner pilots) ends. It assumes the readiness gates in `readiness.md` are met.

## Phases

### Phase 0: Ready (1–2 weeks)
Finish the setup checklist in `readiness.md`: domain and email, analytics, Search Console, CRM, LinkedIn page, legal details, directory profiles, the lead-magnet template, and 4–6 launch content pieces. Turn pilot quotes into proof on the site (with permission).

### Phase 1: Soft launch (weeks 1–4)
- Invite the "Get product updates" list and interviewees **in batches of 10**, with a personal email each. Watch activation closely; fix onboarding friction between batches.
- Founder LinkedIn: an announcement post ("Roveboard is open: here's why I built it"), then 2–3 posts a week.
- Outbound starts small: 10–15 personal contacts a day (see `outbound.md`).
- Concierge onboarding for every new company (offer to import their spreadsheet).
- **Exit criteria:** 10+ activated companies, an activation rate of 40%+ of sign-ups, and the first paying customers (if billing is live).

### Phase 2: Public launch (weeks 5–8)
- Publish the SEO foundations and content (use-case pages, comparison page, template page, first blog posts).
- A launch week: LinkedIn post plus founder's network asks; a newsletter; relevant community posts (within each group's rules); directory listings go live; optional Product Hunt (a bonus for links and credibility, not a customer channel for travel operators).
- Pitch 3–5 travel-trade media and newsletters with an insight angle (see `seo.md` links).
- Ask happy customers for reviews on G2 and Capterra.

### Phase 3: Growth engine (week 9 onwards)
Run the weekly routine below, measure, and double down on what converts. Add channels one at a time. Consider paid channels only when trial → paid works and the customer value is known.

## The 90-day plan (summary)

| Weeks | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Readiness setup | Domain, email, analytics, CRM, profiles, template, launch content |
| 3–6 | Soft launch | Batches of 10 invites, 10–15 outbound/day, 2–3 LinkedIn posts/week, concierge onboarding |
| 7–8 | Public launch | SEO pages live, launch week, directories, media pitches, reviews |
| 9–12 | Engine | 2 blog posts/month, LinkedIn routine, outbound sequences, trial emails, monthly GEO prompt log, first channel review |

## Weekly founder routine (about 8–10 hours a week on growth)

| Day | Block | What |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 1 h | Weekly growth review (template in `templates.md`); plan the week's posts and outreach |
| Daily | 30–45 min | Outbound: 10–20 personal contacts plus replies; LinkedIn comments |
| Tue / Thu | 30 min | LinkedIn post |
| Wed | 2–3 h | Content: one blog post every 2 weeks, or a refresh, or a lead magnet |
| Fri | 1 h | Customer calls and onboarding follow-ups; ask for reviews and referrals |
| Monthly | 1–2 h | SEO (Search Console), GEO prompt log, channel review, newsletter |

## Choosing between channels

Score each channel idea 1–10 on **Impact** (customers if it works), **Confidence** (evidence it will work for *this* ICP) and **Ease** (time and money). Do the highest score first. Give each experiment a hypothesis ("10 operator conversations from 100 LinkedIn connection requests in 3 weeks"), a cost limit and a review date. Kill or keep based on the number.

**Default order for Roveboard:** founder-led outbound (LinkedIn first) + concierge onboarding → founder LinkedIn content → the template + SEO foundations → review sites → partnerships/associations → newsletter and trial emails → events → paid ads (last).

## Budget (phased; say when money starts)

| Stage | Monthly | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-revenue | ~£10–40 | Domain (yearly), Google Workspace (1–2 users), free tiers of everything else |
| First customers | ~£50–150 | A second sending domain and mailboxes, maybe Sales Navigator, a sequencing tool |
| Proven funnel | Up to ~20% of new monthly revenue | Paid tools (SEO suite), events, small paid tests (LinkedIn or Google Ads on high-intent terms), review-site promotion |

Never pay for fake reviews, followers, links or "guaranteed rankings".

## Metrics

**Funnel (weekly):** visitors → trial sign-ups → activated (trip + 3 suppliers + a deadline) → paid. Also conversations held, walkthrough calls, and lost reasons.

**Business (monthly):** MRR, number of paying companies, trial → paid rate, churn, average revenue per company, CAC by channel (once spending money), and payback months.

**Early targets (adjust with real data, then record the actuals here):**
- Activation: 40%+ of sign-ups
- Trial → paid: 15–25% of activated trials (B2B no-card trials often convert lower; measure)
- First 90 days after the public launch: 20–30 paying companies is a strong result for a niche B2B tool with founder-led sales
- Churn: under 3% a month after the first 6 months

**North-star metric:** the number of trips with all suppliers confirmed and no overdue payments each week. It measures the customer's success, not just logins.

## When to revisit positioning

If after ~100 targeted conversations fewer than 1 in 5 operators shows real pain (they share a spreadsheet, ask when they can start or introduce a colleague), revisit the ICP or the message before spending more on channels. Record what changed in `decisions.md`.
